# The comparison form — v0.4's reference altValues for GoMetaRetro (R-V04B-36; stage 4 of the v0.4 build, 2026-10-02)

**IS.** One content-keyed TEXT form per corpus input: the evaluated altValues of GoMeta Core (BLS and the GoMeta Engine — the owner's parenthesis) for that input, together with the content of the BLS Component tree, extracted from their delivered format into text, so that a variant (GoMetaRetro beside v0.4) or a later version is compared with v0.4 BY THE VALUES THEMSELVES — never by bytes, handles, ordinals, ids or Julia types. In the owner's words of 2026-09-29 (OWNER-WORDS_docs §D50 (1.3)): *"The evaluated altValues are of course the yardstick for what GoMeta Core (BLS and GoMeta Engine) produce. However, since the format in which it get returns may change depending on GoMeta's version/variant, it is NOT NECESSARILY possible to compare that output on a bit-level … One could compare the evaluated altValues themselves, however e.g.: extract them from their formats and compare them."* This form is that extraction; the twelve files beside this page, `<stem>.cmp.txt`, are v0.4's forms of the corpus inputs of R-V04B-25 (3b) — the seven under `examples/InFileFolder/` and the five under `tests/golden/constructed/InFileFolder/`. In his terms they are **reference altValues** (his term, given with his hedge *"or something similar"*, §D50 (3.2); the register's OQ-190), NOT "reference files" — a reference file is an input file with the corresponding OUTPUT file of an Extension, in most cases the dev-tool renderer's (§D49 item 2, §D50 (3.1)); the seven committed renders under `examples/OutFileFolder/` are reference files in that sense, the other of his multiple yardsticks (§D50 item 2).

**DOES.** v0.4's adapter is the development tool `comparisonForm` (`devtools/devtools.jl` §I; the writer `writeComparisonFormToFile`; the command-line verb `comparison`); the driver `tests/comparison/regenerate.jl` writes or checks the twelve; the first comparator is `devtools/compare_forms.sh`, a sorted-text diff of two forms (R-V04B-36 (d)). GoMetaRetro produces the same form through its OWN adapter (R-V04B-36 (c); his §70 item 2.2) — this page is the contract; the retro copy (`GoMetaRetro/references/COMPARATOR-FIELDS.md`) is its draft, and a change on either side is proposed to him once and lands in both. Where this page departs from the draft, the retro copy takes it over: the File's span is derived from its Blocks (the draft: "the whole input, no span is written"); every line opens with its tag `C` or `A`; a Segment's span is in characters; no content flag is emitted today. (The children's keys on the Component line are the draft's own field, not a departure — the close seat's F-1, 2026-10-02.)

**REASONING.** The two variants may deliver different shapes and Julia types (his §70 (1)), and even successive versions of one line need not deliver the evaluated altValues in the same format (§D51); only a projection keyed by content can be compared. **PURPOSE.** The twelve forms; the comparison of a retro delivery against them.

**THE FREEZE (his word of 2026-09-29, §D52 → R-DOC-31; his word of 2026-10-03, OWNER-WORDS §304 item 2 — R-V04B-94).** The forms are built with today's behaviour and FROZEN as GoMetaRetro's target by his typed word at this stage's review and landing — NO separate per-question word (the content questions of §4 are judged on the branch's code where he did not rule and by his rulings where he did). The review's word was given on 2026-10-03: *"I approve the freeze."* — the twelve forms as landed at `53bed82e` are the target, three of them as re-made by stage 5's E1 on his word of 2026-10-05 (§324; R-V04B-109 — `heading_quoted_first`, `file_for_Example_Extended`, `file_for_Example_Proposal_JuliaCon`; GoMetaRetro's target moves with them); the landing's word CAME with the branch's merge (7A): his typed word of 2026-10-14, OWNER-WORDS §399 of the build record — `LAND v04-build 91c4b02c` — ratifying the merge of `cc/v04-delivery` into `v04-build` (a fast-forward at `13adba74`, 2026-10-14 14:36) together with the text cures landed over it. Each file's header names the v0.4 commit the writing commit built on — its parent (§2); a changed body is re-made, never edited, in the commit whose change moved it (§5).

## 1. "Same content" (the definition — one paragraph; the owner corrects it)

Two deliveries have the same content for an input when, after each is projected to the form of §2 and its lines are sorted by code point, the two texts are equal (their header lines aside). The form has one line per Component — its kind, its span, its parent's key, its position among the parent's children, its children's keys in child order, its content type and the flags set — and one line per Component per Alterant — the resolved value and its SOURCE, the source being the DECIDING Component's own key (its kind and span) or the level the value came from (`user`, `file`, `default`). Ordinals, ids, handles and Julia types never appear. The File has its Component line and NO answer line (§4, Q10).

## 2. The grammar (v1 — fixed by what v0.4's serializer emits; retro's adapter copies it byte for byte)

- **The file.** Line 1 is the header: `# GoMeta comparison form v1 | <input name> | produced by GoMeta v0.4 at <short sha> | <n> Components | <m> answer lines` — the sha names the commit the writing commit BUILDS ON, its parent (a commit cannot carry its own sha; the forms' birth named `b28dc93b`, the serializer's commit, and landed as the next one; a re-made form names the re-making commit's parent the same way, that commit's message naming the form). Every further line is a form line; the form lines are SORTED by code point (`LC_ALL=C sort`); the file ends with one newline. The header is not content: the comparator drops `#`-led lines.
- **Fields** are separated by ` | ` (space, vertical bar, space). A text value is quoted (below), so a bar inside a text never splits a field.
- **A Component's KEY** is `<kind> <span>`: `File 1-12`, `Block 3-7`, `Line 4-4`, `Segment 4:1-12`.
  - *kind*: `File`, `Block`, `Line`, `Segment`.
  - *span*: a Block `a-b` and a Line `n-n` in INPUT-LINE numbers (1-based); a Segment `n:s-e` — its line's number, a colon, then its span WITHIN the line in CHARACTERS (1-based, inclusive; v0.4's parser records byte offsets, converted on the line's own bytes — the session's choice of the character unit, his to confirm, §4); the File `a-b` from its first Block's start line to its last Block's stop line (the File's own locator fields are the parser's dummy; `0-0` for an input without a Block).
- **The Component line.** `C | <kind> | <span> | <parent's key, or -> | <position among the parent's children, 1-based, or -> | <children's keys in child order, joined by ;, or -> | <content type: Meta, Code, Text; - for the File> | <flags>`.
  - *flags*: the flags of his approved table that are TRUE on the Component, each qualified by its group — `component.<name>` for the ComponentSettribute flags EXCEPT `hide`, `show`, `discard` (Visib in the tree is not compared, his §69 item 5; `depth0`–`depth9` and the two file-writing flags `includeInOutFile`, `write` included until his word), `content.<name>` for the content flags — sorted by code point, joined by `,`; `-` when none. (v0.4 emits no `content.<name>` flag today: the content flag vector has no read through the BLS door and no writer in the parser — §4.)
- **The answer line.** `A | <kind> | <span> | <Alterant> | <value> | <source>` — one per registered Alterant (the default registry: `Visib`, `labels`, `id`, `heading`, written as registered) for every Block, Line and Segment; none for the File.
  - *value*: `null` for no value; `true`/`false`; a Symbol as its bare name (`show`); a text double-quoted with the escapes `\\`, `\"`, `\n`, `\r`, `\t`; an integer in decimal; a SET as `{a,b}` with the members' texts sorted by code point (`{}` empty); a COMPOUND as `(k=v,k=v)` with the fields sorted by name. No Julia type or container name.
  - *source*: the deciding Component's key — the Component's OWN key when its own statement decided (`:own`), the key of the Component the answer came THROUGH when inherited (`:indirect` — the metaBlock that an attached Block and the metaBlock's own Lines follow, a metaLine split by a trailing comment included since stage 5's E1; the Line a rowless Segment answers through; R-V04B-93); `user` for the fed profile, `file` for the File level (no feed at v0.4), `default` for the Alterant's declared default (R-V04B-55, R-V04B-92). Three fallback arms of the serializer lie outside this grammar and are reached by NO delivery of the default registry over the twelve inputs (measured): a bare `indirect` (an answer inherited through a Component the delivery cannot name), `none` (a custom registry's Alterant without a declared default) and a quoted `repr` of a value of an unforeseen type; the battery's §18 flags the first two.
- **The content type** is the Component's flavor as BLS names it (`kind`): `Meta`, `Code`, `Text`.

## 3. What is NOT compared

Component ids and ordinals; cell handles and the rows' keys; the `range` struct field (written by neither parser); the `hide`/`show`/`discard` flags; Julia types and containers; the order of lines (sorted); the header line (each lane's producer).

## 6. Using this yardstick from another variant or version (GoMetaRetro, GoMetaRetroCC, a later GoMeta) — his order of 2026-10-03 (OWNER-WORDS §304 item 1)

The yardstick is the FORM of §2; the twelve files beside this page are version 0.4's reference altValues in that form, FROZEN as the target on his word of 2026-10-03 (§304 item 2; R-DOC-31). A variant uses it so:

1. **The same inputs.** The twelve corpus inputs are the seven `examples/InFileFolder/*.jl` and the five `tests/golden/constructed/InFileFolder/*.jl` of THIS code home at the commit each form's header names (`produced by GoMeta v0.4 at <sha>`). Run your line on the same bytes — read them in place or copy them unchanged; never edit them (they are his examples, the law of the design).
2. **Your own adapter** (R-V04B-36 (c)): one function in your line's development tools that takes your delivery for one input and writes the form of §2 — the header line with your line's name in place of `GoMeta v0.4` and your producing commit, then every C and A line, sorted by code point (the order of `LC_ALL=C sort`), one newline at the end. The grammar is fixed here: copy it byte for byte — the separator ` | `, the key shapes, the span rules and the character unit, the flag qualification and sorting, the value grammar, the source words. A field your line cannot yet produce (the source as a Component key before your delivery records an origin, say) is written as `-`, and a header comment line (`#`-led, dropped by the comparator) says which field and why — so the comparison shows the gap at the body lines, honestly, instead of hiding it.
3. **Your twelve** go into a folder of YOUR line (`tests/comparison/` there, the same stems, `.cmp.txt`); nothing is written into this folder but by this line's `regenerate.jl`.
4. **Compare file by file:** `bash <this code home>/devtools/compare_forms.sh <this line's form> <your form>` — exit 0 = the same content; exit 1 = different, the differing lines printed (`<` this line's, `>` yours); exit 2 = usage. A difference in a `C` line is a difference in the parser's reading of the file (the tree's content); in an `A` line, in the Engine's evaluated value or in where it came from.
5. **Read a difference against §4 before changing code.** The points listed there are his to decide; a variant does not settle them by matching version 0.4, and version 0.4 does not settle them by having landed first.
6. **When this line's forms are re-made** (a later word of his moves a value), the new headers name the re-making commit's parent and that commit's message names what moved and why; re-run step 4 against the new files.
7. **Record the result as the comparator's output, unabridged** (the canon's R4b), in your line's record — never a retyped summary of it.
